# Possible papers (hypotheses, not commitments)

1. **The institutional structure of contemporary Hungarian sociology.** Data: full phase-1
   coverage of institutions and units. Contribution: first census-like map; concentration,
   Budapest vs regional centres, units outside formally named sociology departments.
2. **Academic genealogy and reproduction in Hungarian sociology.** Data: doktori.hu
   supervision records, positions, academy membership. Contribution: school reproduction
   measured by documented supervision rather than reputation.
3. **The computational turn in Hungarian social science.** Data: dated methods from
   publications/projects, CSS-RECENS and Lendület groups, co-authorship. Contribution:
   where computational methods entered, through whom, and whether network science and
   CSS are separate lineages.
4. **The structure and historical transformation of Hungarian Roma research.** Data: topic
   edges, projects, institutions (Pécs Romology, TK, Miskolc, ELTE), co-authorship.
   Contribution: test the one-community assumption.
5. **Centre and periphery: Budapest and regional academic networks.** Data: city of
   institutions, cross-institution ties over time.
6. **Intellectual fragmentation and brokerage.** Data: topic/method bipartite network +
   collaboration. Contribution: who bridges theory/empirical and disciplinary divides.

Each requires the dataset release it uses to be cited with its `release_id`, manifest and
quality report.
